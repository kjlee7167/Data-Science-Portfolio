# Video Game Sales Prediction

Forecasting video game sales trends across regions.

## Overview

Analyzed historical sales data across North America, Europe, and Japan to identify genre trends and predict future sales.

---

## Methods

- EDA
- Time Trend Analysis
- Linear Regression

---

## Technologies

R  
ggplot2  
dplyr

---

## Results

- Action dominated NA and EU
- RPG strongest in Japan
- Built regional sales forecasting models
