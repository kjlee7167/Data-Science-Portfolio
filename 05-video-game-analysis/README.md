# Video Game Sales Analysis

Exploring regional video game sales trends and genre popularity using exploratory data analysis.

## Overview

This project analyzes historical video game sales data across North America, Europe, and Japan to identify regional market preferences and long-term industry trends.

The analysis explores which genres performed best across regions and examines how sales patterns changed over time alongside console generations.

---

## Objective

- Explore regional differences in video game sales
- Identify genre popularity trends
- Analyze historical market changes
- Investigate relationships between release timing and sales

---

## Dataset

Video Game Sales Dataset

Variables analyzed:
- Year of Release
- Genre
- North America Sales
- Europe Sales
- Japan Sales

Dataset size:
- 16,719 observations

## Key Findings

- North America generated the highest overall sales volume
- Action games were most popular in North America and Europe
- Role-Playing games showed strongest popularity in Japan
- Video game sales increased significantly after major console generations
- Forecasting suggested genre popularity may continue influencing future regional sales
