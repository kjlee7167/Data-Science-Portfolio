# DataFest 2026 — Patient Journey Delay Analysis

Healthcare analytics project focused on understanding factors associated with delayed patient care journeys.

## Overview

This project was completed during DataFest 2026 and analyzed large scale patient datasets to identify characteristics associated with delayed healthcare journeys.

The analysis focused on understanding how visit patterns, diagnosis groups, demographics, and healthcare utilization related to delays in care.

---

## Objective

- Define patient journey delay
- Identify factors associated with delayed care
- Compare delayed vs non-delayed populations
- Generate actionable healthcare insights

---

## Dataset

DataFest Healthcare Dataset

Sources:
- Patients
- Encounters
- Diagnoses
- Departments

## Key Findings

- Delayed journeys represented a minority of total journeys
- Higher journey complexity was associated with increased delays
- Diagnosis groups showed different delay patterns
- Visit behavior and department utilization differed between delayed and non-delayed patients
